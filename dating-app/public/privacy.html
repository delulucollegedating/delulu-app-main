Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

Delulu ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, college email address, date of birth, gender identity, and profile information
  • Profile Content: Photos, bio, hobbies, and preferences
  • Messages: Chat messages sent through the app (encrypted end-to-end)
  • Verification: College email verification data

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage, session duration
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, crash reports, error logs
  • Location Data: General location (city/region) for matching purposes (not precise location)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the Delulu service
  • Match you with other students at your college
  • Send you notifications about new connections and messages
  • Verify your college student status
  • Improve app features and user experience
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and ensure safety
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Analyze usage patterns and app performance

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 We Share Information With:

  • Other Users: Your profile information (name, photo, bio) is visible to other verified students at your college
  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate (Firebase, Supabase, Railway, Brevo Email)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales

3.2 We DO NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your messages with anyone (they are end-to-end encrypted)
  • Share your precise location data
  • Use your data for advertising to you

4. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All messages are encrypted before leaving your device
  • HTTPS: All data transmission uses secure SSL/TLS encryption
  • Access Controls: Limited employee access to personal data
  • Screenshot Protection: App prevents screenshots of sensitive content
  • Secure Storage: Data stored in secure, encrypted databases

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 You Can:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct: Update your profile information in the app
  • Delete: Request account deletion (see Account Deletion section)
  • Opt-Out: Disable push notifications in your device settings
  • Export: Request export of your data

5.2 Account Deletion

To delete your account:

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  2. Confirm deletion
  3. Or email us at: support@delulu.app

Upon deletion, we will:

  • Remove your profile from discovery
  • Delete your personal information within 30 days
  • Retain only anonymized usage data for analytics
  • Keep data required for legal compliance (e.g., reports of abuse)

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Needed to provide services
  • Required by law (e.g., financial records, abuse reports)
  • Necessary to resolve disputes or enforce agreements

After account deletion, most data is deleted within 30 days, except:

  • Legal compliance data (up to 7 years)
  • Anonymized analytics data (indefinitely)
  • Backup data (up to 90 days)

7. Children's Privacy

Delulu is intended for college students aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

We use these third-party services that may collect information:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, push notifications, database
  • Supabase: Message storage and real-time updates
  • Railway: Application hosting
  • Brevo: Email delivery
  • Sentry: Error tracking and crash reporting

Each service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in compliance with applicable laws.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell)
  • Right to deletion of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising rights

To exercise these rights, email: privacy@delulu.app

11. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights under GDPR:

  • Right of access, rectification, erasure, and data portability
  • Right to restrict or object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by:

  • Posting the new policy in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email or in-app notification for significant changes

Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
