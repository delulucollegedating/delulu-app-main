⚠️ Warning: This action is permanent

Once you delete your account, all your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

What will be deleted:

  • Your profile and account information
  • All your messages and chat history
  • Your connections and matches
  • Your photos and uploaded content
  • Your preferences and settings

Before you delete:

  • Make sure you've saved any important conversations or information
  • Consider whether you might want to return to Delulu in the future
  • If you're having issues with the app, contact support first—we're here to help
