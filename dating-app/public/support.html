📖 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

To permanently delete your account:

  1. Open the Delulu app
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Tap "Account"
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm deletion

Or email us at support@delulu.app with "Delete My Account" in the subject line.

Note: Account deletion is permanent. Your profile, matches, and messages will be deleted within 30 days.

I'm not receiving notifications. What should I do?

Try these steps:

  1. Check permissions: Settings → Notifications → Enable
  2. Check Do Not Disturb: Disable DND mode
  3. Restart the app: Force close and reopen Delulu
  4. Update the app: Check for updates in the Play Store
  5. Check battery optimization: Allow Delulu to run in background

Still not working? Email support@delulu.app with your device model and Android version.

How do I report someone?

In-App Reporting:

  1. Open the user's profile
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮)
  3. Tap "Report"
  4. Select the reason and add details
  5. Submit report

Email Reporting: safety@delulu.app

Include:

  • Username of the person you're reporting
  • Description of the issue
  • Screenshots (if applicable)
  • Date/time of incident

We review all reports within 24-48 hours.

How do I block someone?

To block a user:

  1. Open their profile
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮)
  3. Tap "Block User"
  4. Confirm

When you block someone:

  • They cannot see your profile
  • They cannot send you messages
  • You won't see them in discovery
  • Existing connections are removed
Why can't I verify my college email?

Make sure:

  • You're using a .edu email address
  • The email belongs to an accredited institution
  • Check your spam/junk folder for the verification email
  • Wait a few minutes and try resending

If your college email still doesn't work, contact us at support@delulu.app with:

  • Your college name
  • Your email domain (e.g., @stanford.edu)
How does the identity reveal work?

Delulu uses a gradual reveal system:

  • Day 1-6: Anonymous profiles (no names, no photos)
  • Day 7: Identity Reveal - names visible
  • Day 10: Face Reveal - photos visible
  • Day 11: Connections expire after 24 hours if no interaction

This encourages authentic conversations before physical appearance.

Are my messages encrypted?

Yes! All messages are end-to-end encrypted (E2EE). This means:

  • Only you and your match can read messages
  • Delulu cannot read your messages
  • Messages are encrypted before leaving your device
  • Even if our servers were compromised, messages remain secure
Can I use Delulu at multiple colleges?

No, your account is tied to one college ecosystem. This keeps the experience local and authentic to your campus community.

If you transfer schools, email support@delulu.app to update your college affiliation.

What data does Delulu collect?

We collect:

  • Profile information (name, bio, photos)
  • College email for verification
  • Usage data (features used, session duration)
  • Device information

We DO NOT collect:

  • Your message content (encrypted)
  • Precise location data
  • Browsing history outside the app

Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

The app is crashing or buggy. What should I do?

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  1. Update the app: Check Play Store for updates
  2. Clear cache: Android Settings → Apps → Delulu → Storage → Clear Cache
  3. Restart device: Power off and on
  4. Reinstall: Uninstall and reinstall Delulu (your account is saved)

Still having issues? Email support@delulu.app with:

  • Device model and Android version
  • Description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the bug
  • Screenshots (if applicable)
